Larry Williams Volatility Breakout Strategy on Bitcoin: 451 Trades, -61% After India's 30% Crypto Tax

11 Nov 2026 · Updated 14 Nov 2026 · Backtest Bharat

The Larry Williams volatility breakout strategy returned +5.3% on Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, over 451 trades. After India's 30% crypto tax, it lost 61.2%.

Larry Williams turned $10,000 into more than $1.1 million in a 12-month trading contest in 1987. His best-known idea, the volatility breakout, fits in one line, so we ran that line on 3 years of Bitcoin.

Key takeaways
  • Volatility breakout buys when today's price rises above the open by half of yesterday's range, and sells at the day's close.
  • On Binance BTC/USDT, Nov 2023 to Nov 2026 (0.1% fee), ₹1 lakh became ₹1,05,300. Simply holding Bitcoin made it ₹2,05,400.
  • After India's 30% crypto tax, the same 451 trades left only ₹38,800. Holding kept ₹1,73,800.

Who is Larry Williams?

  • He won the 1987 World Cup Championship of Futures Trading with a net return of 11,376%, a real-money contest that runs for 12 months
  • In his own words, he took "$10,000 to $1.1 million in 12 months"
  • Ten years later his daughter Michelle won the same contest with 1,001%
  • He says he created the volatility breakout in 1982

▶ Larry Williams on the 1987 contest and how he trades. Source: TraderLion (YouTube, 2021)

The contest record shows only the return. It does not say which method he used in 1987, and Williams credits money management for much of that result. So this post tests the breakout idea itself, not the 1987 account.

Volatility breakout strategy by Larry Williams: the 4 rules

1
Measure yesterdayRange = yesterday's high minus yesterday's low
2
Set today's buy levelBuy level = today's open + 0.5 × range
3
Buy on the breakoutIf price touches the buy level during the day, buy there
4
Sell at the closeSell at the end of the same day, win or lose

The 0.5 is called k. Williams describes the idea as buying "some expansion" of the range added to the open, so k is a setting, not a law. 0.5 is a commonly used value.

On Binance the daily candle starts at 00:00 UTC, which is 5:30 AM in India. That is when "today's open" is fixed.

His own verdict: "It has not worked as well in recent years." Source: Larry Williams, ireallytrade.com

Why India's 30% tax turns +5% into -61%

  • Profit from crypto (VDA) is taxed at a flat 30%, plus surcharge and cess. The rate is the same for a 1-day trade and a 3-year holding
  • A loss on one sale cannot reduce the tax on another, and cannot be carried forward
  • This rule sits in Section 194 (Table, item 4) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It was Section 115BBH under the old Act

The actual rule: 30% rate, and losses cannot be set off or carried forward. Source: Income Tax Department, Govt of India

The 205 winning days are taxed one by one. The 246 losing days are ignored. So the tax bill is calculated on a profit the account never really had.

A strategy that wins small and often is the worst possible match for this tax rule. The Turtle Trading test, with only 31 trades, kept +11.7% after the same tax.

1% TDS on 147 sales a year

The law also asks for 1% TDS on crypto sales. Indian exchanges deduct it at the time of sale. You get credit for it when you file your return, but until then the money is not in your trading account.

This strategy sold 147 times in FY 2025-26. If 1% is taken from every sale, 0.99 multiplied 147 times is 0.23. Before any profit or loss, about 77% of the capital would be sitting as TDS credit by March.

Our backtest does not subtract this cash lock, so the real result would be worse. Check how your own exchange applies TDS.

FAQ

What is the volatility breakout strategy by Larry Williams?

It is an intraday volatility breakout strategy: buy when today's price rises above the open by half of yesterday's range, and sell at the close of the same day. On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, this rule made 451 trades and returned +5.3% before tax. Williams says he created it in 1982.

Does the Larry Williams volatility breakout strategy work on Bitcoin?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, it returned +5.3% over 451 trades with a 45.5% win rate, far below buy and hold (+105.4%). After India's 30% crypto tax it lost 61.2%. With zero fees the same trades returned +159.6%. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

What is k in the volatility breakout formula?

k is the share of yesterday's range that price must rise above today's open before you buy. This test uses k = 0.5, a commonly used setting.

Is intraday crypto trading taxed differently in India?

No. Income from the transfer of a virtual digital asset is taxed at 30% whatever the holding period, and losses cannot be set off. With that rule, ₹1 lakh fell to ₹38,800 in this test.

What time does the daily Bitcoin candle start in India?

Binance daily candles start at 00:00 UTC, which is 5:30 AM IST. Today's open and yesterday's range are fixed at that time.

Did Larry Williams win the 1987 championship with this strategy?

The official standings record only his 11,376% return, not the method. Williams says he created the volatility breakout in 1982 and that his contest result was largely due to money management.

Do I get the 1% TDS on crypto back?

TDS is a credit against your final tax. If the TDS deducted is more than the tax you owe, the extra is refunded after you file your income tax return.
