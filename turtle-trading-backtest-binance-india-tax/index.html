Turtle Trading Strategy Rules and Backtest: Does It Still Work on Bitcoin After India's 30% Crypto Tax?

11 Nov 2026 · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · Backtest Bharat

Does the Turtle Trading strategy still work? On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, it returned +49.2% before tax and +11.7% after India's 30% crypto tax. Simply holding Bitcoin returned +105.4% and +73.8%.

If you trade crypto in India, every winning trade gives 30% to the taxman, and your losing trades give nothing back. That one rule changes which strategies are worth your time, so we tested the most famous trend-following system, Turtle Trading, and counted the tax.

Key takeaways
  • Turtle Trading buys when price breaks the 20-day high and sells when it breaks the 10-day low.
  • On Binance BTC/USDT, Nov 2023 to Nov 2026 (0.1% fee), ₹1 lakh became ₹1.49 lakh. Simply holding Bitcoin made it ₹2.05 lakh.
  • After India's 30% crypto tax, Turtle Trading kept only ₹1.12 lakh. Holding kept ₹1.74 lakh.

What is the Turtle Trading strategy by Richard Dennis?

A set of rules that Chicago trader Richard Dennis taught to 23 people he hired through newspaper ads in 1983 and 1984.

  • The ad said trading experience was "considered, but not necessary"
  • The group averaged close to 80% a year over about four and a half years (The Wall Street Journal, 1989)
  • The name came from a turtle farm Dennis saw in Singapore: "We are going to grow traders just like they grow turtles in Singapore"

▶ Richard Dennis and two original Turtles, Jerry Parker and Brian Proctor, tell how the experiment started. Source: Top Traders Unplugged (YouTube, 2018)

Turtle Trading strategy rules: the 4 rules we tested

1
EntryBuy when price breaks above the 20-day high
2
Position size1 unit = the amount where a move of N changes the account by 1% (N = 20-day average true range)
3
StopSell everything if price falls 2N below the last entry
4
Add and exitAdd 1 unit every ½N up (max 4 units). Exit all when price breaks the 10-day low
This test is spot only with no borrowing, so it usually stops at 2 to 3 units.

Why Indian crypto tax hits this strategy hard

  • Profit from crypto (VDA) is taxed at a flat 30%, plus surcharge and cess. Only the purchase cost can be deducted
  • A loss on one sale cannot reduce the tax on another, and cannot be carried forward
  • Since April 1, 2026 this rule sits in Section 194 (Table, item 4) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It was Section 115BBH under the old Act

The actual rule: 30% rate, and losses cannot be set off or carried forward. Source: Income Tax Department, Govt of India

Before tax, holding was ahead by 56 percentage points. After tax, the gap grew to 62. For Indian traders, the tax rule matters as much as the entry rule.

The hidden cost: 1% TDS on every exit

Exchanges also deduct 1% TDS each time you sell crypto. You get it back as a credit when you file your return, but the money is stuck until then.

In this test, the Turtle trader sold 31 times. 1% TDS on those sales adds up to about 32% of the starting capital over 3 years, around 10% a year. On ₹1 lakh, that is roughly ₹10,000 a year waiting for a refund instead of trading.

Our backtest does not subtract this cash lock, so real results would be a little worse. HODL pays TDS once, at the final sale.

Turtle trading rules on Nifty 50: 10-year backtest

On the Nifty 50 index from Aug 1, 2016 to Jul 31, 2026, with a 0.1% cost on each buy and each sell, the basic Turtle entry and exit turned ₹1 lakh into ₹1,53,947. Simply holding the index turned it into ₹2,81,470.

What we tested: daily prices of the Nifty 50 index (^NSEI) from Yahoo Finance, 2,464 trading days. Buy when the day's high goes above the highest high of the previous 20 sessions, at that level (or at the open if the day opens above it). Sell when the day's low goes below the lowest low of the previous 10 sessions, at that level (or at the open if the day opens below it). Long only, all in or all out, cash earns nothing. No 2N stop, no unit sizing, no pyramiding and no skip-after-a-winner filter, so this is simpler than the 4-rule version tested on Bitcoin above. Holding buys at the first open and pays the 0.1% cost once.

What is Turtle Trading?

Turtle Trading is a set of trend-following rules that Chicago trader Richard Dennis taught to 23 people he hired through newspaper ads in 1983 and 1984. It buys when price breaks the 20-day high and sells when it breaks the 10-day low. On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, these rules returned +49.2% before tax in our test.

What are the Turtle Trading strategy rules?

The version tested here on Binance BTC/USDT daily candles has 4 rules. Entry: buy when price breaks above the 20-day high. Position size: 1 unit is the amount where a move of N changes the account by 1% (N is the 20-day average true range). Stop: sell everything if price falls 2N below the last entry. Add and exit: add 1 unit every ½N up (max 4 units) and exit all when price breaks the 10-day low.

What is the Turtle Trading strategy win rate?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, 7 of 31 trades won, a win rate of 22.6%. The average win was +17.2% and the average loss was -5.0%.

How does India's 30% crypto tax change the Turtle Trading result?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, ₹1 lakh grew to ₹1,11,700 after tax instead of ₹1,49,200 before tax. The model takes 30% tax on each winning trade with no set-off for losses. Holding Bitcoin, taxed once at the end, grew to ₹1,73,800.

Is Binance legal in India?

Binance registered with FIU-IND as a reporting entity in 2024, after a penalty of ₹18.82 crore for operating without registration. Its website and app have been available in India since then. Tax and TDS rules still apply to your trades.

Do I get the 1% TDS on crypto back?

TDS is a credit against your final tax. If the TDS deducted is more than the tax you owe, the extra is refunded after you file your income tax return.
