About Backtest Bharat

Backtest Bharat in one line: we take trading strategies people talk about, run them on real Bitcoin data, and show what is left after fees and India's 30% crypto tax.

Why this blog exists

Most strategy videos show the winning trades. Very few show the losing streaks, the fees, or the tax.

In India, the tax part is big. Every profitable crypto trade is taxed at 30%, and a loss on one trade cannot reduce the tax on another. A strategy that looks good on a chart can look very different after that.

What every post includes

  • The exact rules of the strategy, from the original book or source
  • A backtest on Binance BTC/USDT daily data, usually 3 years and 1 year
  • Fees of 0.1% per trade, and results before and after India's 30% tax
  • Every losing trade counted, plus the worst drop (max drawdown)
  • Sources and the date we last checked the numbers

What we do not do

  • No buy or sell calls, no coin tips, no price targets, no paid groups
  • We are not registered with SEBI and do not give investment advice. Posts are for education only

Who runs it

Backtest Bharat is an independent research blog run by a retail trader-researcher based in Seoul, South Korea. It is not owned by or affiliated with any exchange or broker.

Backtests are written in Python. Drafts are prepared with the help of AI tools, and every number in a post comes from our own code and data files.
