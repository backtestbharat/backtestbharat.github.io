Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Backtest Bharat is hosted on GitHub Pages. This page explains what data is collected when you visit.

GitHub Pages

GitHub, which hosts this site, logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics script and no ads.

Embedded charts and videos

Posts embed a TradingView chart and YouTube videos (in privacy-enhanced mode). When they load or play, TradingView and Google receive standard visit data such as your IP address and browser, and may set their own cookies. Their privacy policies apply.

Earlier posts on Blogger

Our earlier posts are on backtestbharat.blogspot.com, which is hosted on Google Blogger. There, Blogger and Google may use cookies and collect standard visit data such as IP address, browser and pages viewed, for statistics, security and, if enabled, ads. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Country check for referral links

Some posts check your country so that referral links are shown only where they are allowed. Your browser asks Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or GeoJS for a country code. We do not store this result or your IP address.

Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate or referral link, the partner site (for example TradingView or Binance) may set its own cookies to track the referral. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

Comments and contact

This site has no comment form. If you contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository, GitHub processes your account name and message, and the issue is public. We use it only to reply.

Children

This blog is not intended for anyone under 18.

For education only, not investment advice. Backtests use past data and do not guarantee future returns. We are not SEBI registered.

Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such transactions.
