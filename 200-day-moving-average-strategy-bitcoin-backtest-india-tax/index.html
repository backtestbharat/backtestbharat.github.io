200 Day Moving Average Strategy on Bitcoin: 200 DMA Win Rate and Backtest After India's 30% Crypto Tax

13 Nov 2026 · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · Backtest Bharat

The 200 day moving average strategy returned +88.2% on Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, against +105.4% for holding. But it sat in cash for 289 days while Bitcoin fell 45%, and its worst drop was -31.6% against -53.0% for holding.

Paul Tudor Jones founded the Tudor Group in 1980. In October 2025 he told CNBC that "nothing good ever happens under the 200 day moving average in any asset". We ran that one line on 3 years of Bitcoin.

Key takeaways
  • The 200 day moving average strategy: hold Bitcoin when the daily close is above its 200-day average (200 DMA), hold cash when it is below.
  • On Binance BTC/USDT, Nov 2023 to Nov 2026 (0.1% fee) it made 15 trades with a 33.3% win rate. ₹1 lakh became ₹1,88,200. Simply holding Bitcoin made it ₹2,05,400.
  • In the last 1 year the rule made +8.5% while holding lost -28.0%.
  • After India's 30% crypto tax the rule kept ₹1,48,700. Holding kept ₹1,73,800.

What is the 200 DMA?

  • DMA means day moving average. The 200 DMA is the average of the last 200 daily closing prices
  • Every day the newest close is added and the oldest one is dropped, so the line moves slowly
  • Price above the line is read as a long-term uptrend. Price below it is read as a downtrend

Type "200 dma" into Google in India and it suggests "200 dma means" and "200 dma of nifty 50". Type "200 day moving average strategy" and it suggests "200 day moving average bitcoin strategy". This post tests that last one.

Who is Paul Tudor Jones?

  • Paul Tudor Jones II founded the Tudor Group in 1980 and is its Co-Chairman and Chief Investment Officer
  • In May 2020 he told CNBC that he had "just over 1% of my assets in bitcoin" and called it "a great speculation"
  • In an interview printed in Tony Robbins' 2014 book Money: Master the Game, he is quoted as saying: "My metric for everything I look at is the 200-day moving average of closing prices."
  • In the same interview: "You play defense, and you get out."

His own words on CNBC, Oct 6, 2025. Source: CNBC transcript, cnbc.com

▶ Paul Tudor Jones on how he views bitcoin (2 minutes). Source: CNBC Television (YouTube, 2020)

Jones has not published this as a trading system. He calls the 200-day moving average his metric for the trend and a way to play defense. So this post tests the simplest reading of his sentence: above the line hold, below the line cash. It is not a test of how he or his fund trades.

The loss was the first of four. Between Mar 13 and Mar 29, 2025 the price crossed the line again and again, and the rule bought and sold four times. All four trades lost: -3.26%, -1.87%, -3.21% and -2.12%, about -10.1% together.

That is the weak point of this strategy. When the price sits on the line, the rule keeps buying a little higher and selling a little lower.

A 33.3% win rate sounds bad. It works because the average winner (+20.31%) is almost nine times the size of the average loser (-2.34%). Winners were held 133 days on average, losers 3 days.

The uncomfortable number: 10 of the 15 trades lost money. Two trades, +55.13% and +21.93%, made the result. The other 13 trades together returned -0.5%.

The biggest winner also started on day one. On Nov 10, 2023 Bitcoin was already 28.5% above its 200-day line, so the rule bought at the first open. A test that starts on a different day gets a different first trade.

Fees were a small part of the story. With zero fees the result is +94.0% instead of +88.2%, because the rule trades only about 5 times a year. The 9 20 EMA crossover test shows what happens when the same kind of rule trades far more often.

What the rule is for: 289 days in cash

  • The highest daily close in the test was $124,659 on Oct 6, 2025
  • On Nov 3, 2025 Bitcoin closed below the line. The rule sold the next morning at $106,583, 14.5% under that high
  • It then stayed in cash for 289 days. The lowest close in that time was $58,625 on Jun 30, 2026, 45.0% below the selling price
  • It bought back on Aug 20, 2026 at $69,335, 18.3% above that low and 34.9% cheaper than it had sold

The rule does not sell the top and does not buy the bottom. It gives up the first part of every fall and the first part of every rise. What it avoids is the middle of a long fall.

The price of that protection is the 10 small losses. Together they cost -21.1%. That is why the rule still finished behind holding over the full 3 years.

After India's 30% crypto tax

  • Any income from the transfer of a virtual digital asset is taxed at 30%. The Table lists no separate rate for long holdings
  • No set-off of a loss is allowed, and the loss cannot be carried forward to later tax years
  • This rule sits in Section 194 (Table, item 4) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Income-tax Act, 1961 had the same rule in Section 115BBH

The actual rule: 30% rate, and losses cannot be set off or carried forward. Source: Income Tax Department, Govt of India

Holding loses exactly 30% of its profit. The rule lost about 45% of its profit. The 5 winners are taxed one by one, the 10 losers give no relief, and tax paid early cannot grow in later trades.

So the gap gets wider after tax: ₹1,48,700 for the rule against ₹1,73,800 for holding. The holding figure assumes you sell once at the end and pay tax once.

1% TDS on 10 sales a year

The Income Tax Department's guide says tax is deducted at 1% of the sale consideration, and that when you sell through an exchange which pays you directly, the exchange deducts it. Its TDS FAQ says you claim the credit of that tax in your return of income.

This rule sold 10 times in FY 2024-25 and 4 times in FY 2025-26. If 1% is taken from every sale, 0.99 multiplied 10 times is 0.904. Before any profit or loss, about 9.6% of the capital would be sitting as TDS credit by the end of the busier year.

Our backtest does not subtract this cash lock, so the real result would be a little lower. Check how your own exchange applies TDS.

200 DMA vs 200 EMA

Google in India also suggests "200 ema strategy win rate" and "200 dma vs 200 ema". An EMA gives more weight to recent prices, so it turns a little faster. We ran the same rule with a 200 EMA, and with shorter simple averages.

The 200 EMA made more trades, won fewer of them and had a deeper worst drop (-38.3% against -31.6%). The difference is small, and one 3-year period cannot settle which line is better.

The 50-day line did best in this window. That does not make 50 the right number. Picking the best setting after seeing the results is called curve fitting. After tax, none of the four lines beat holding.

Does the 200 DMA rule work on Nifty 50? 10-year backtest

On the Nifty 50 index from Aug 1, 2016 to Jul 31, 2026, with a 0.1% cost on each buy and each sell, the same 200 DMA rule turned ₹1 lakh into ₹1,77,942. Simply holding the index turned it into ₹2,81,470.

What we tested: daily closes of the Nifty 50 index (^NSEI) from Yahoo Finance, 2,464 trading days. If the day's close is above the simple average of the last 200 closes (that day included), the rule holds the index from the next day's open. If the close is at or below the average, it holds cash from the next day's open. Cash earns nothing. Long only, all in or all out, no stop, no leverage. Holding buys at the first open and pays the 0.1% cost once.

FAQ

What is the win rate of the 200 day moving average strategy?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, 5 of 15 trades won, a win rate of 33.3%. The average winner made +20.31% and the average loser lost -2.34%. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

What does 200 DMA mean?

200 DMA means 200-day moving average: the average of the last 200 daily closing prices. A price above the line is read as a long-term uptrend, and a price below it as a downtrend.

Does the 200 day moving average strategy work on Bitcoin?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, it returned +88.2% against +105.4% for holding, with a worst drop of -31.6% against -53.0%. In the last 1 year it made +8.5% while holding lost -28.0%. This is one coin and one 3-year period, not a general rule.

Does the 200 day moving average strategy work on Nifty?

Its worst drop was -19.5% against -38.4%. 13 of 39 trades won (33.3%). Dividends, fund costs and tax are not included. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

200 DMA vs 200 EMA: which is better?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, the simple 200 DMA made 15 trades and returned +88.2%. The 200 EMA made 20 trades and returned +80.6%. After India's 30% tax they kept +48.7% and +37.2%. One period cannot settle it.

How does India's 30% crypto tax change the 200 day moving average result?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, 30% tax on each winning trade and no set-off for losses, ₹1 lakh ended at ₹1,48,700 instead of ₹1,88,200. Holding Bitcoin, taxed once at the end, ended at ₹1,73,800 instead of ₹2,05,400.

Did Paul Tudor Jones publish the 200-day moving average rule as a trading system?

No. He has called the 200-day moving average his metric for the trend and said he gets out below it. This post tests the simplest reading of that sentence, not how he or his fund trades.

What time should I check the 200 DMA for Bitcoin in India?

Binance daily candles close at 00:00 UTC, which is 5:30 AM IST. The close and the 200-day average for the day are fixed at that time.

Method and sources

  • Data: Binance BTC/USDT daily candles (00:00 UTC), Nov 10, 2023 to Nov 9, 2026. Candles from Jun 1, 2022 were used to warm up the 200-day average. Fee 0.1% per side, long only, spot, no leverage, no slippage, full capital on each trade
  • Rule details: simple average of 200 daily closes, signal day included. Signals use the closed daily candle and orders fill at the next day's open. On the first day the position is set by the previous day's close. The position still open on Nov 9, 2026 is closed at that day's close and counted as the 15th trade
  • A second, separately written calculation on a separate data download gave the same 15 trades, +88.2% before tax, +48.7% after tax and -31.6% worst drop
