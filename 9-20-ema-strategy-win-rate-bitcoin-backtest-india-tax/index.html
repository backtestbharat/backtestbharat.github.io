9 20 EMA Strategy Win Rate: Bitcoin Backtest on 4 Timeframes, After India's 30% Crypto Tax

12 Nov 2026 · Updated 14 Nov 2026 · Backtest Bharat

The 9 20 EMA strategy win rate on Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, was 21.7% on the 15-minute chart, 27.2% on 1-hour, 31.1% on 4-hour and 36.4% on daily.

Type "9 20 ema strategy" into Google in India and it suggests: win rate, accuracy, time frame, backtesting. So we ran the rule on 3 years of Bitcoin, on four different charts. The same rule made ₹1 lakh into ₹1.98 lakh on the daily chart and into ₹1,300 on the 15-minute chart.

Key takeaways
  • Win rate: 21.7% on the 15-minute chart, 27.2% on 1-hour, 31.1% on 4-hour, 36.4% on daily. It never reached 40%.
  • After fees (0.1% per side): only the daily chart made real money, +98.3% in 3 years. Holding Bitcoin made +105.4%.
  • After India's 30% crypto tax: daily kept +39.8%. The 4-hour chart went from +9.1% to -43.1%.

What is the 9 20 EMA strategy?

  • An EMA (exponential moving average) is an average of recent closing prices that gives more weight to the latest candles
  • The 9 EMA follows price closely. The 20 EMA moves more slowly
  • When the fast line crosses above the slow line, the strategy buys. When it crosses back below, it sells

Google in India also suggests "9 20 ema strategy stock burner". Stock Burner is a Hindi YouTube channel. In his video the example trades are on 1-hour and 15-minute charts, with a stop-loss below the swing low and targets chosen by judgment.

▶ Hindi: the 9-20 EMA setup explained with live crypto trades. Source: Stock Burner (YouTube, 2025)

Stops and targets chosen by judgment cannot be backtested. So this post tests the base rule that every version is built on: the crossover itself. It is not a test of any one trader's personal method.

The rule we tested

1
Two lines9 EMA and 20 EMA of closing prices
2
BuyWhen a candle closes with the 9 EMA above the 20 EMA after being below it, buy at the next candle's open
3
SellWhen a candle closes with the 9 EMA back below the 20 EMA, sell at the next candle's open
4
Nothing elseNo stop-loss, no target, no filter. Spot only, full capital on each trade

We ran this exact rule on 15-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour and daily candles of Binance BTC/USDT.

Why the short charts lost

The 15-minute chart with and without trading costs

  • With zero fees, the 15-minute chart returned +112.5%. With a 0.1% fee on each buy and each sell, it returned -98.7%
  • 2,545 trades means 5,090 fee payments. Each round trip costs 0.2% of the account
  • The average 15-minute winner made +1.31% and the average loser lost -0.57%. With only 21.7% winners, that does not cover the cost

After India's 30% crypto tax

After tax, only the daily chart is still in profit

  • Profit from crypto (VDA) is taxed at a flat 30%, plus surcharge and cess. The rate is the same for a 15-minute trade and a 3-year holding
  • A loss on one sale cannot reduce the tax on another, and cannot be carried forward
  • This rule sits in Section 194 (Table, item 4) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It was Section 115BBH under the old Act

The actual rule: 30% rate, and losses cannot be set off or carried forward. Source: Income Tax Department, Govt of India

The 4-hour chart is the clearest case. It made a small profit before tax. But its 46 winning trades are taxed one by one and its 102 losing trades give no relief, so a +9.1% result becomes -43.1%.

Even on the daily chart, tax took more than half of the profit: ₹1,98,300 before tax, ₹1,39,800 after. Holding kept ₹1,73,800.

1% TDS by timeframe

The law also asks for 1% TDS on crypto sales. Indian exchanges deduct it at the time of sale. You get credit for it when you file your return, but until then the money is not in your trading account.

The last column is simple arithmetic: 0.99 multiplied by itself once per sale, before any profit or loss. Our backtest does not subtract this cash lock, so real results on the short charts would be worse. Check how your own exchange applies TDS.

FAQ

What is the win rate of the 9 20 EMA strategy?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, the plain 9 20 EMA crossover won 21.7% of trades on the 15-minute chart, 27.2% on 1-hour, 31.1% on 4-hour and 36.4% on daily. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

Which time frame is best for the 9 20 EMA strategy?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, only the daily chart made real money (+98.3% in 3 years). The 1-hour and 15-minute charts lost. This is one coin and one 3-year period, not a general rule.

What is the 9 20 EMA strategy accuracy?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, no timeframe won more than 36.4% of its trades. On the daily chart the profit came from two large winners. A low win rate with a few big wins is normal for crossover strategies.

Does the 9 20 EMA strategy work on a 15-minute chart?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, it made 2,545 trades on the 15-minute chart and lost 98.7%. With zero fees the same trades returned +112.5%. The fees decided the result.

How does India's 30% crypto tax change the 9 20 EMA result?

On Binance BTC/USDT from Nov 2023 to Nov 2026, with a 0.1% fee per side, 30% tax on each winning trade and no set-off for losses, the daily chart fell from +98.3% to +39.8% and the 4-hour chart from +9.1% to -43.1%. Holding Bitcoin, taxed once, fell from +105.4% to +73.8%.

Is this the Stock Burner 9-20 EMA strategy?

No. This post tests the base crossover rule. His version adds a stop-loss below the swing low and targets chosen by judgment, and judgment cannot be backtested.

Method and sources

  • Data: Binance BTC/USDT candles (15-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour, daily, UTC), Nov 10, 2023 to Nov 9, 2026. Fee 0.1% per side, long only, spot, no leverage, no slippage, full capital on each trade
  • EMA: standard formula with multiplier 2 / (length + 1), started from a simple average. Signals use closed candles and orders fill at the next candle's open. A position still open on Nov 9, 2026 is closed at the last price
